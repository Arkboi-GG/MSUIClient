## Protocol and state increment

Build-5875 server source was checked before implementation. Banker activation
uses a full packed object GUID and returns `SMSG_SHOW_BANK`. Bank item movement
uses the ordinary inventory swap body across player slots 39–62; bank bag slots
are 63–68. `PLAYER_BYTES_2` byte 2 is the purchased bank-bag-slot count.

The successful bank-slot purchase handler does not emit
`SMSG_BUY_BANK_SLOT_RESULT`: it deducts the DBC price and increments the update
field. Error paths alone return the result packet. The instrument therefore
records success only from the independent money and slot-count deltas.

The client now has bounded bank state, exact open/buy bodies, deposit and
withdraw actions, bank-slot pricing from DBC, a bank UI, copyable verdicts,
runner commands, and bank byte/state checks.
