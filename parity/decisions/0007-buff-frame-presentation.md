# 0007 — Preserve MSUI Buff Frame presentation

- Registry entry: ui/buffframe
- Date: 2026-08-23
- Decided by: Nico

## Benilla behavior
Benilla uses its authored aura anchor, row spacing, timer, tooltip, and stack typography.

## MSUI behavior (preserved)
MSUI keeps its established anchor, 35-pixel rows, compact timer style, tooltip placement, and stack typography.

## Why
Nico explicitly placed Buff presentation on today's no-touch list.

## Boundaries
Aura data, ordering, duration, cancellation, hover ownership, lifecycle, and protocol state remain must-match.
